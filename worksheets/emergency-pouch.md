# Emergency Pouch Sheet (blank)

Two copies: one in the pouch, one in the pouch backup. This is the thirty-second grab page —
everything an ER registrar or a new nurse might request in the first hour, filled out by hand,
kept current. Store the pouch by the door.

Photo of this sheet in your password-protected phone storage **only if** you update the photo
whenever the sheet changes. Group chats and email are not storage.
