# Document Locator (blank)

paper physically lives. Never write passwords, full account numbers, or card digits on this
page — locations only, kept somewhere you control.

Replacements, if lost: Social Security card → SSA; Medicare card → 1-800-MEDICARE; birth
certificate → the state's vital records office; ID → the DMV. For anything legal that is
missing when a signature can no longer be made, call an elder-law attorney promptly — it is
time-sensitive. Coordination help, never medical, legal, or tax advice.
