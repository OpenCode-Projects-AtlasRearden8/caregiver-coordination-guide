# Disclaimer

**Short version:** this is a coordination manual. It helps you organize paperwork, calls,
logs, families, and letters. It is **never medical advice**, never a diagnosis, never a
treatment plan, and never a substitute for any professional — medical, legal, tax, or
financial.

**Longer version:**

- **What this guide is.** Practical, educational help with the *logistics* of caring for an
  aging or ill loved one: what documents to find, who to call, how to keep records, how to
  divide family tasks fairly, and how to use AI tools to draft updates you review and send.
- **What it is not.** It is not medical care, diagnosis, treatment, triage, or advice about
  any medication, symptom, procedure, or end-of-life decision. Those belong exclusively to
  your loved one and their qualified clinicians, possibly along with those the person has
  legally designated (a health-care agent, for example).
- **Medication logs, doctor logs, and worksheets** record what actually happened. They never
  decide what should happen. If any worksheet seems to suggest a dosage, a schedule beyond
  what a prescriber wrote, or any clinical judgment, that is a formatting coincidence — ask
  the prescriber or pharmacist, and go by *their* answer.
- **Emergency situations are handled by emergency services.** Symptoms that feel like an
  emergency go to local emergency numbers, not to this guide, a spreadsheet, or an AI chat.
- **Legal, tax, and financial topics** (powers of attorney, wills, benefits, facility
  contracts, insurance appeals) are mentioned here as logistics pointers only. Rules differ
  by state and country and change over time; use qualified professionals. Your local Area
  Agency on Aging can point you to low-cost help first.
- **AI-generated drafts** are drafts. You proofread every one for accuracy, kindness, and
  privacy before sending. AI can get facts confidently wrong; the person who sends the
  letter owns its contents — which is one reason you, not the chat, press "send."
- **Privacy.** Keep personal health information out of group chats and out of AI prompts
  except in the minimal forms the [AI updates chapter](06-ai-weekly-updates.md) suggests.
  Nothing in this guide asks you to send anyone's identifying details anywhere.
- **People differ.** What organizes one family may not fit another. Adapt the worksheets,
  bin what does not fit, and trust the person you are caring for — they outrank every
  chapter here.
